## Revised Recommendation

**The short version:** Start at roughly 50/50 unless your situation clearly calls for something different. Measure what's working. Shift toward whatever is generating real traction — but measure the two channels differently, because they operate on different timescales.

### Before you start: Adjust your baseline

50/50 is the right default **if you're unsure**. But override it immediately if:
- **You're senior/executive-level or in a relationship-driven field** (consulting, sales, leadership roles): Start at 70/30 networking. At your level, roles often aren't posted publicly.
- **You're entry-level, switching fields, or have a thin network**: Start at 60/40 toward applications. You need to build networking muscle before it can carry your search, and cold outreach converts poorly without shared context.
- **You have 30+ warm contacts you can reach out to today**: Start at 60/40 networking. You have a head start.

### Do Now

1. **Set your starting split based on the guidance above and begin this week.** For most people at ~20 hours/week, that's roughly 10 and 10. Don't overthink it — directional is fine.

2. **Track results lightly, but measure each channel on its own terms.** One simple note at the end of each week:
   - *Networking:* How many outreach attempts → how many turned into real conversations? (This is your conversion rate. If you're converting less than ~20% of genuine outreach attempts into conversations after 2-3 weeks, your approach needs fixing before you scale it.)
   - *Applications:* How many sent this week? (Don't expect callback data for 4-6 weeks. For now, just track volume and quality — are you finding roles worth tailoring for?)

3. **At the 4-week mark, make your first real adjustment.** Not before. You need networking conversion data *and* enough time for application callbacks to start arriving. When you adjust, ask two questions:
   - Is networking producing conversations that feel *useful* — meaning you're learning about real opportunities, getting clearer on your positioning, or generating referrals? (Not just pleasant chats.)
   - Are applications generating any callbacks or movement?
   
   Shift toward whichever channel is producing real forward motion. If both are working, stay balanced.

**Consider Later:**
- Formal conversation quality scoring — useful if you're networking heavily and want to sharpen your pitch, but unnecessary in the first month
- Detailed time categorization for overlapping activities — don't worry about whether a referral-driven application "counts" as networking or applications; the goal is outcomes, not accounting

**Skip:**
- Rigid hour-counting between categories — aim for a rough split, not a stopwatch
- Any fixed numeric threshold like "2+ conversations" independent of your total hours invested — what matters is your *conversion rate* on outreach, not an absolute number

### The one-sentence version

Split your time evenly to start (unless your seniority or network size clearly says otherwise), measure networking by conversion rate and applications by volume for now, and don't judge applications until you've given them 4-6 weeks to produce callbacks.
